You are a clinical decision extractor.

Read the discharge note below and extract every in-hospital physician decision made during this admission.

A physician decision is an active attending choice made IN RESPONSE to clinical information observed during the admission:
- Ordering a drug for the first time (not continuation of pre-admission medications)
- Stopping or switching a medication in response to new findings
- Performing or directing a procedure
- A key management choice documented in the notes that involves a specific drug or a specific procedure

Every decision MUST be classifiable as either medication or procedure:
- medication: any action involving a specific named drug (starting, stopping, switching, adjusting dose)
- procedure: any action involving a specific named operation, intervention, or therapeutic procedure (surgery, dialysis, catheterization, tube placement, etc.)

If a decision does not involve a specific drug name or a specific procedure name, do NOT extract it.

NOT a decision — do not extract these:
- Background history or reason for admission
- Clinical findings, lab results, vital signs (these are inputs, not decisions)
- Routine nursing care, wound dressing changes, or protocol steps
- Ordering a diagnostic test (MRI, CT, EEG, labs) — only the management response counts
- Pre-admission planned procedures (e.g. elective surgery that is the reason for admission)
- Admission or transfer decisions (e.g. "admitted to ortho service", "transferred to ICU")
- Discharge planning, discharge instructions, follow-up appointments, or disposition decisions
- Routine prophylaxis that applies to all inpatients (e.g. DVT boots, standard heparin prophylaxis, Protonix for GI prophylaxis) unless it represents a non-routine attending choice
- Decisions framed as NOT doing something or choosing inaction — these have no positive action to predict
- Vague or abstract management plans without a specific drug or procedure (e.g. "conservative management", "observation")
- Continuing or maintaining a medication that is already established and running during the admission — only extract the initial start, stop, or switch decision
- Nutritional plans, diet orders, or nursing protocols (unless they involve a specific named drug or procedure)

Extract decisions in STRICT CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER as they occur during the admission.
- Earlier decisions must appear before later ones — follow the temporal sequence of the hospitalization
- If the note is not written chronologically, reconstruct the timeline from clinical context clues
- Focus on decisions where the clinical context in the timeline would be sufficient to predict the decision — skip decisions that are purely narrative or administrative

Output JSON only:

    {"id": 0, "description": "...", "type_hint": "medication|procedure"},
    {"id": 1, "description": "...", "type_hint": "medication|procedure"},
    ...
  ]
}
