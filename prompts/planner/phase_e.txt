## Phase E — Final output

Output the final plan as a single JSON object. No markdown fences, no extra text — JSON only.

GT source types:
- {"source": "icd_diagnosis", "icd_code": "...", "icd_version": 9|10, "display": "..."}
- {"source": "icd_procedure", "index": N, "icd_code": "...", "icd_version": 9|10, "display": "..."}
- {"source": "medication", "index": N, "drug": "..."}
- {"source": "note_section", "section": "...", "span": "..."}

available_agents rules — list only the agents whose information is essential for the doctor to make the decisions required at this stage:
- "patient": include when direct patient history or symptom questioning is needed (typically Stage 1 and any stage where new patient-reported symptoms drive the decision)
- "nurse": include when bedside vitals, monitoring data, fluid balance, or administered medications are needed
- "lab": include when laboratory results or diagnostic imaging are needed
- "history": include when prior admission records would meaningfully inform this stage's decisions
