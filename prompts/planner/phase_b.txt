## Phase B — Identify key physician decisions

Call get_timeline_index() and get_note_section("Brief Hospital Course") to understand the full admission.

Your task: find every key physician decision made during this admission, in chronological order. These decisions will become the GT for each stage — so find them BEFORE defining any stage boundaries.

**What counts as a key decision:**
- A new procedure or intervention (use icd_procedure GT — find the exact event index)
- A medication newly held or stopped due to a clinical event (use note_section GT from BHC)
- A medication newly started or restarted after being held (use note_section GT from BHC)
- Discharge diagnosis summary (use icd_diagnosis GT — call get_diagnoses)

**What is NOT a key decision:**
- Daily pharmacy re-orders of the same drug (administrative, not clinical)
- Routine maintenance medications continued unchanged
- Lab values, vitals, imaging findings (inputs to decisions, not decisions)

**For each decision, record:**
1. Clinical description (what the physician decided)
2. Event index (for icd_procedure: the exact index; for note_section: N/A)
3. GT citation (icd_procedure with index, OR note_section with exact BHC text, OR icd_diagnosis code)

Use get_events_by_index or search_events to locate procedure events and verify indices.

Output a numbered decision list before proceeding to Phase C. The last decision is always the discharge diagnoses (call get_diagnoses).
