## Phase C — Build ground truth

For each stage, identify what the physician decided or ordered. Ground truth is the physician's action — not what the patient reported and not what a test or imaging study showed.

### Citation preference (use the highest applicable tier)

**Tier 1 — ICD diagnosis code** (preferred for diagnosis stages):
Call get_diagnoses to retrieve ICD codes. Use this when the GT is a diagnosis the physician established.
  {"source": "icd_diagnosis", "icd_code": "...", "icd_version": 9|10, "display": "..."}

**Tier 2 — ICD procedure code** (preferred for procedures and interventions):
Procedure events in the timeline (source_table: hosp_procedures_icd_df) carry icd_code and long_title_procedure. Use this when the GT is a procedure or intervention the physician ordered.
  {"source": "icd_procedure", "index": N, "icd_code": "...", "icd_version": 9|10, "display": "..."}

**Tier 3 — Medication name** (preferred for drug therapy decisions):
Pharmacy/prescription events carry a standardized drug name. Use this when the GT is a medication the physician initiated for this stage's clinical problem.
  {"source": "medication", "index": N, "drug": "..."}

**Tier 4 — Note section span** (fallback only):
Use this only when the physician's decision cannot be expressed by any of the above (e.g. "decided not to operate", "family goals-of-care discussion"). The span must describe a physician action, not a patient symptom or test result.
  {"source": "note_section", "section": "...", "span": "<exact text from tool result>"}

### Rules
- Every GT item must come from a tool call return value in this session. Do not write GT from memory.
- Lab values, vital signs, and imaging findings are NOT valid GT.
- GT must be directly relevant to the clinical problem being managed in this stage. Do not cite pre-admission maintenance medications (e.g. chronic antihypertensives, statins, vitamins) that were continued unchanged.
- A stage may have multiple GT items if the decision had multiple components.

Call get_events_by_index, search_events, or get_diagnoses as needed to locate evidence.

Write out the GT items for each stage before proceeding.
